DMAUT AI Platform — User Account Information
===============================================

User Session & Data Isolation:
- Every user has their own secure, isolated workspace session.
- To access the platform, sign up or log in from the main screen at http://localhost:5173.
- All new accounts start completely fresh with clean, empty sessions.
- Users can choose either a "Business" or "Personal" account type during registration.

Account Types:
1. Business Account:
   - Full access to all 6 modules: AI Copy Generator, Multi-Channel Campaign Manager, CSV RFM Customer Segmentation, Event-Driven Automations with live simulations, A/B Campaign Optimizer, and Advanced Analytics.

2. Personal Account:
   - Streamlined interface for creators and solo marketers: AI Copy Generator, Simple Campaign Manager, and Reach Analytics.

Account Management:
- Access your profile, edit username, change email, update password, switch account tiers, or delete your account at any time via the Account menu located at the bottom of the sidebar.
